The speech model runs on your Mac, so what you say stays on your Mac. murmur is free and open source (MIT).

murmur is based on [Handy by cjpais](https://github.com/cjpais/Handy) and keeps its MIT licence (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). murmur adds its own look (wave icon, colour themes, glass recording bar), a smart prompt capsule that can turn a long dictation into a clear AI prompt or tidy it up, and a quiet microphone boost so soft speech is still heard.

## Install

Download **[murmur.dmg](https://github.com/Solomon-69/murmur/releases/latest/download/murmur.dmg)** (always the newest), open it and drag murmur into Applications. Or paste one line in Terminal: `curl -fsSL https://solomon-69.github.io/Mac-Apps/download/install.sh | bash -s murmur`. murmur checks for updates itself and shows an Update button when a new version is out.

## Use

1. Click into any text box.
2. Hold Option and Space, speak, and let go.
3. Your words appear where the cursor is.

You can change the shortcut on the General page in Settings, and choose between holding it or tapping it once.
